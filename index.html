  1. No satellite photographed the sea in 1960, so the 1960 area is from the cited study and the yellow line is NASA's approximate 1960 shoreline. Satellite images: NASA Earth Observatory, Terra MODIS true-colour scenes of the Aral Sea, 25 August 2000, 15 August 2001 and 21 August 2018, with the approximate 1960 shoreline in yellow. All three share one frame.
  2. Aral Sea area: 1960 (68,000 km²) and 2010 from Applied Water Science, 2012; 1998, 2004, 2018 and North Aral values from Wikipedia, Aral Sea. Different studies, so approximate.
  3. Global temperature: NASA GISTEMP v4, annual anomaly vs 1951–1980. Carbon dioxide: NOAA GML, Mauna Loa annual mean. Both via open datasets packages.
  4. Tracker factors: UK government greenhouse gas conversion factors 2024: average petrol car 0.1645, local bus 0.10846 and national rail 0.03546 kg CO₂ per km, as listed by Durham University. Shower flow of 9 L/min is an assumption.
  5. Methods: Mann-Kendall test, Sen’s slope with 95% range, and trend-free pre-whitening when years depend on each other. A trend is not a cause.
